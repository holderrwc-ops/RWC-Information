# Revolutionary Warfare Command Verification Bot

This is the verification bot for the **Revolutionary Warfare Command** aka RWC Roblox group and its Discord server.

To link their accounts, members run /verify in our Discord server and choose Log in with Roblox. Linking gives them access to the server and lets their event XP count towards ranks in our Roblox group. The bot only receives their Roblox username and user ID, never their password, email or any other account details.
