# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 30 September 2026*

This policy explains what the Revolutionary Warfare Command verification bot ("RWC Automation") collects and how it is used.

## What we collect

When you run `/verify` and choose **Log in with Roblox**, Roblox tells the bot your:

- **Roblox user ID**
- **Roblox username**

The bot never receives your Roblox password, email address or any other account information.

The bot also stores:

- your **Discord user ID**, to link your Discord account to your Roblox account;
- the **XP** you earn at our events, and a **history of XP and rank changes** (when, how much, and which staff member made the change).

## How we use it

Only to:

- track your XP;
- give you the matching rank in our Roblox group;
- give you the matching roles in our Discord server.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

## Login tokens

The login tokens Roblox gives the bot are used **once** to read your user ID and username. The long-lived refresh token is revoked immediately, and the short-lived access token is thrown away. Tokens are never stored.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while you take part in our community. Backups of the data are kept by the server administrators for safety.

When you verify, your Discord account and Roblox username are posted in a private staff channel, so the community's staff can see which Discord account belongs to which Roblox account.

Your Roblox username, XP and rank can be seen by other members of the Discord server through the leaderboard, the /profile command and promotion announcements.

To have your data removed, or to unlink your account, contact the server admadministrators in our Discord server.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
